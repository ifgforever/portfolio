Frequently asked questions

Common questions

How fast can a lead actually get a response? +

Seconds, not minutes — the automation replies the moment the inquiry comes in, 24 hours a day, including nights and weekends when nobody's watching the phone.

Will customers know they're talking to AI at first? +

The first reply is typically framed as an automated confirmation, not a person pretending to be human. Once a lead is qualified, a real person takes over — this replaces the delay before contact, not the relationship itself.

What happens to leads that aren't a good fit? +

They're filtered out before they take up your time — routed to a polite decline, a referral, or simply logged, depending on what you want. You only get pinged for the ones worth your attention.

Can this work alongside my existing CRM? +

Yes. Lead automation is usually the front door — it captures and qualifies, then hands off into whatever CRM or booking system you already run. See CRM optimization if that system also needs cleanup.
