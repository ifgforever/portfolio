Frequently asked questions

Common questions

Will this feel like spam to my customers? +

Not if it's built right — the messages are tied to something that actually just happened (a booking, a completed job), not a blast to your whole list. That context is what makes it feel like service, not marketing.

Can I approve the message wording before it goes live? +

Yes, and you should. Every message template is written to sound like you, then reviewed and approved before any automation is turned on.

What channels does this send through — text, email, both? +

Whichever your customers actually respond to. Most small service businesses see better response on text for reminders and follow-ups, with email as backup for anything longer.

What happens when a customer replies? +

Replies route to a real person, not back into the automation. This is meant to start a conversation reliably, not carry on a full conversation on its own.

How is this different from a generic email marketing tool? +

Most email tools require you to build and remember to send campaigns. This is triggered by real customer events — a booking, a completed job, a stale quote — so the right message goes out without anyone remembering to click send.
